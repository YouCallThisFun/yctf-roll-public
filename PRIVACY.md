# Privacy Policy for THE ROLL - You Call This Fun

Effective date: August 30, 2026

THE ROLL - You Call This Fun (the “Extension”) is a public, read-only Twitch Extension operated by You Call This Fun.

## Information You Call This Fun collects

You Call This Fun does not collect, store, sell, share, or receive personal information from viewers through the Extension.

The Extension:

- does not request access to a viewer's Twitch identity;
- does not read or write Twitch chat;
- does not use analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking pixels;
- does not ask viewers to create an account or submit information; and
- performs searches, sorting, and entry expansion locally in the viewer's browser.

## Public archive information

The Extension displays the public champion archive for the You Call This Fun game show. Public records may include an official Roll number, a champion's public display name, an episode label, and an inscription time. The Extension does not receive or display game questions, answers, private evidence, Twitch user IDs, email addresses, or broadcaster credentials.

## Third-party services

Twitch hosts and displays the Extension. GitHub serves the sanitized public archive file used by the Extension. When a viewer uses Twitch or retrieves the public archive, Twitch and GitHub may automatically process standard network or device information, such as an IP address, browser information, or server logs, under their own privacy policies. You Call This Fun does not receive or control those third-party logs.

- [Twitch Privacy Notice](https://www.twitch.tv/p/en/legal/privacy-notice/)
- [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## Children's privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other viewers.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the Extension's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a material update is published.

## Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or correction requests may be submitted through the [You Call This Fun public support repository](https://github.com/YouCallThisFun/yctf-roll-public/issues).
